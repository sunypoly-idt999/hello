You used "I don't understand this. Give me a different explanation," and stopped after the model's first re-explanation. Two things were required and missing: a second move from the list, and a second push on the first move.

This matters more than it looks, because Ex7 and Ex8 both instruct you to "use one of the two moves from Exercise 3." With only one move established, every subsequent challenge in the portfolio is the same move — Ex7 turn 2 and Ex8 turn 4 are both "I don't understand this." You never deployed *my experience tells me otherwise*, *this reading says something different*, or *here's another model's response* as a named move.

You did do experience-based pushback organically in Ex5 and Ex6 — the Clippy comparison, the agent skepticism, the challenge about invisible adaptation as a first step toward dark patterns. So the capability is clearly there. What's missing is the deliberate, labeled use of it.

**To revise:** rerun Ex3 with a second move, and record in the artifact which two you used.

You have `transcripts/`, `artifacts/`, `synthesis/`, `author/`, and `zip/`. The model didn't know that, because you didn't tell it, and you didn't correct it when it invented a competing structure. The exercise is about organizing *this* workbench, not designing a new one.

**To revise, and do this before starting Ex12:** issue the missing prompt, tell the model the actual five-folder structure, and have it advise on that.
